# Product Steering

Build Voice-Based Appointment and Task Assistant for End users, accessibility-focused users, service operators. The core problem is: Users need hands-free task and appointment workflows that reliably interpret speech, maintain conversational state, and confirm consequential actions.

Prioritize a usable, safe end-to-end workflow over broad feature count. Keep AI outputs reviewable, evidence-aware, and measurable. Avoid claims of production readiness without test evidence.
