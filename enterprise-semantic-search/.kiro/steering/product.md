# Product Steering

Build Context-Aware Enterprise Search Engine for Employees, knowledge managers, search administrators, developers. The core problem is: Keyword-only search misses intent and context, while unranked semantic results can reduce precision and trust.

Prioritize a usable, safe end-to-end workflow over broad feature count. Keep AI outputs reviewable, evidence-aware, and measurable. Avoid claims of production readiness without test evidence.
